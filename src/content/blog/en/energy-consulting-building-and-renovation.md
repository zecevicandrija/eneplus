## 4 Costliest Mistakes Prevented by Energy Consulting

When licensed engineers review building plans before materials are installed and HVAC machinery is ordered, they consistently identify and rectify four high-cost issues:

### 1. Oversized heating and cooling machinery
HVAC distributors frequently operate on a "bigger is safer" mindset, recommending a 16 kW heat pump or 24 kW boiler for a home whose calculated heat loss is only 9 kW.

During transitional seasons (autumn and spring), an oversized machine suffers from severe "short cycling"—turning on and off continuously in brief spurts. This results in:
* Accelerated compressor wear and a drastically shortened equipment lifespan,
* Higher electricity consumption due to repeated startup current spikes,
* Substantial overspending on initial hardware (often €3,000 to €6,000 in unnecessary capital expense).

Through rigorous heat loss calculations in accordance with standard EN 12831, energy consulting determines the exact thermal capacity needed in kilowatts.

### 2. Overlooking thermal bridges and vapor diffusion
Adding thick insulation to an exterior wall is only half the battle. If balcony slabs, concrete ring beams, window lintels, parapets, and roof junctions lack continuous thermal breaks, heat rapidly escapes through these structural pathways.

As indoor moisture encounters these cold internal surfaces, condensation forms, inevitably producing toxic black mold and structural damage. Remedying mold after interior finishing is invasive and costly. Consulting during the design phase specifies thermal separation details, air barriers, and correct vapor-permeable membrane layering.

### 3. Airtight construction without dedicated ventilation
Modern high-performance windows and wall systems seal a building so thoroughly that natural infiltration practically drops to zero. Without planned mechanical ventilation with heat recovery (MVHR), indoor spaces become stale, carbon dioxide ($CO_2$) levels spike, and occupants suffer from lethargy and reduced cognitive function.

When occupants attempt to ventilate manually by tilting windows in winter, up to 45% of generated heat escapes outdoors. Energy consulting balances air tightness with balanced heat recovery ventilation, preserving indoor warmth while maintaining fresh air quality.

### 4. Missing out on green grants and low-interest credit lines
Government ministries, local authorities, and international financial institutions (such as the EBRD GEFF framework) provide substantial non-refundable subsidies (ranging from 30% to 65% of investment value) for high-efficiency windows, heat pumps, insulation, and solar PV installations.

However, grant applications require strict compliance with defined thermal transmittance ($U$-values) and seasonal performance coefficients ($SCOP / SEER$). An energy consultant guides your equipment selection so you do not purchase hardware that fails to meet grant eligibility thresholds.

## FAQ: Energy Efficiency Consulting

#### Why hire an independent energy consultant instead of relying on equipment sellers or contractors?
Equipment sellers are primarily driven to sell larger, more expensive units or stock items they currently have in their warehouse, while building contractors often follow old habits and rules of thumb. Independent certified engineers have zero commercial stake in equipment sales—their sole objective is an exact physical and techno-economic calculation that protects your capital budget and minimizes lifetime operating costs.

#### At which project stage should energy efficiency consulting take place?
The ideal moment is Phase Zero (conceptual design), before the architectural layouts are finalized and prior to purchasing building envelope materials or HVAC equipment. Consulting is equally critical right before launching a comprehensive building retrofit (before replacing windows, facade, or boiler plants) to ensure measures are sequenced correctly.

#### What documents are required to book an energy consultation at Eneplus?
For an initial consultation, it is recommended to provide architectural drawings or sketches (PDF or DWG), technical specifications of existing or planned building envelope layers (walls, roof, floor slabs), details of proposed heating/cooling and ventilation systems, and past 12 to 36 months of utility bills if assessing an existing building.

#### How much money and energy can be saved through professional energy consulting?
Through proper HVAC sizing (avoiding oversized heat pumps and chillers) and thermal bridge elimination, building owners routinely save between 10% and 25% of their total mechanical budget upfront, while long-term utility bills for heating and cooling are reduced by 30% to 50%.

#### Does energy consulting help when applying for green subsidies and bank grants?
Yes. National government funds, municipal energy programs, and international financial institutions (such as EBRD GEFF and EU credit lines) require strict energy performance criteria and certified engineering documentation. Our team guides you through the technical requirements to secure non-refundable grants and green financing.

#### Does energy consulting include photovoltaic (PV) solar system sizing?
Yes. The Eneplus engineering team consists of certified mechanical and electrical engineers. Our consultations cover the complete synergy: heating and cooling load calculations (mechanical engineering) paired with rooftop PV solar generation and prosumer modeling (electrical engineering), including grid connection analysis and payback modeling.
