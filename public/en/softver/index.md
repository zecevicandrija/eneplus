Energy Management Software is a web-oriented software that is an indispensable tool in the work of an energy manager.

### Who is it for?

The software is primarily intended for municipalities, public utility companies, but can also be used by other interested companies that want to monitor their energy consumption and performance.

CAPABILITIES

## Energy Management Software allows us to:



Storage and access to information on energy and water consumption in all facilities covered by the system.



Continuous updating and maintenance of database on individual facilities



Continuous entry and monitoring of consumption data for all types of energy sources and water



Processing and analysis of collected data



Early detection of unnecessary energy waste



Creating an information basis for decision making



Review and analysis of historical operational data



Identification, preparation and execution of energy efficiency improvement projects



Precise definition of consumption, efficiency and performance indicators



Budgeting support



Evaluation of energy efficiency measures
