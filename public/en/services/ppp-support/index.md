# PPP PREPARATION  
AND SUPPORT

ENEPLUS provides expert assistance to the public and private sectors in the preparation and implementation of public-private partnership projects.

### Our services

*   Project identification and definition with option elaboration
*   Market analysis and location selection
*   Preparation of PPP project proposal
*   PPP project preparation (project team, project plan and timeline)
*   Pre-tender phase (studies, PPP model, procurement selection, criteria, draft Agreement)
*   Risk analysis and financial construction
*   Preparation of tender documentation and tender implementation
*   Energy efficiency in procurement
