# ENERGY  
CERTIFICATION

## Energy efficiency elaborate

Determination of the fulfillment of the conditions of energy efficiency of the building is done by the energy efficiency elaborate. It is an integral part of the technical documentation necessary for the issuance of a building permit for the construction of new and reconstruction of existing buildings

## Energy passport

Certificate of energy properties of buildings - energy passport is a document containing the calculated value of energy consumption within a certain category of buildings, energy class and recommendations for improving the energy properties of a building.

Energy passports must have all new buildings, as well as existing buildings that are being reconstructed, adapted, renovated or sanitized, except buildings that are exempted from the requirement of energy certification.

An energy passport is an integral part of the technical documentation that is enclosed with the application for the issuance of a usage permit.
