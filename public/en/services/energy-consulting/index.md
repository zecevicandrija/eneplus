SERVICE

# ENERGY  
CONSULTING

As part of its services, ENEPLUS provides consulting services to all interested legal and natural persons on the construction and reconstruction of buildings in accordance with energy efficiency principles.

### Scheduling an appointment

Appointments are scheduled via email at: [office@eneplus.rs](mailto:office@eneplus.rs)

Please provide the following information in your application:

*   Name and surname, contact phone
*   Brief description of your subject of interest
*   Additional documentation (if any) in electronic form

\* Bring documentation related to your facility to the scheduled consultation.

![Energetski konsalting](/_next/image?url=%2FAssets%2Fencons.avif&w=3840&q=75)

#### Appointment time

Please list all time slots between **10 AM and 3 PM** that suit you during the work week.

We will inform you of your appointment on the same day, no later than 3 PM.
