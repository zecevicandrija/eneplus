## Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding energy efficiency, management, and consulting services

What is the price for an energy passport and how long does it take?−

The cost of an energy passport depends on the size and type of the building. For standard residential properties, the price usually ranges from 15,000 to 30,000 RSD. The preparation typically takes 2 to 5 business days, ensuring a fast and efficient process.

Is an energy passport legally required?+

Yes, according to the Law on Planning and Construction. It is mandatory for all new buildings, as well as existing buildings undergoing reconstruction or adaptation. It is also required when selling or renting out a property.

What does the energy management process entail?+

Energy management is the systematic monitoring and analysis of energy consumption aimed at reducing costs (following ISO 50001). The process includes a detailed energy audit, the development of an energy-saving action plan, and supervision of implemented measures.

How do your energy consulting services work?+

Our energy consulting provides expert advice on optimal solutions for your facility. Simply send us a project description and relevant documentation, and our engineers will analyze and propose the most energy-efficient and cost-effective steps.

Why is an energy audit important before renovation?+

An energy audit deeply analyzes your current condition using state-of-the-art equipment. It precisely locates energy losses before you start renovating, which prevents unnecessary expenses and directs the budget to measures that yield the highest savings.
