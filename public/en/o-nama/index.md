# ABOUT US

ENEPLUS is a consulting firm providing services in the field of energy management, energy efficiency, and project management.

Our expert team is at your disposal for analyzing and selecting optimal solutions that will increase your system's energy efficiency while reducing costs.

ENEPLUS develops strategies and implements solutions that enable process optimization, cost reduction, and sustainable development.

## Expertise, Regulations, and Savings

Our licensed engineers possess extensive experience working across Serbia. Whether you require advanced thermography, aligning systems with guidelines set by the Energy Agency, or preparatory documentation for a municipal inspection, Eneplus guarantees strict adherence to agreements, precise delivery times, and unquestionable quality.

Since the Law on Planning and Construction mandates high standards for building and renovation, obtaining a valid energy efficiency elaborate is a mandatory step toward acquiring official permits. Our firm also handles the official issuance of energy passports, ensuring their mandatory entry and recording through the national BEES registry.

We are fully aware of the importance of financial planning for every project. Therefore, our price of preparation is transparent and fully adapted to the complexity of your facility. Furthermore, we regularly guide our clients on the easiest and fastest ways to obtain currently available state subsidies for energy efficiency.
